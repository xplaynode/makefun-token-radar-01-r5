How the values are derived

Every protocol in the snapshot that already has a token contributes its market-cap-to-TVL ratio. A pre-token protocol is priced off the median ratio of its own category, shrunk toward the global median with six pseudo-peers of weight, clamped to a sane band, and pulled further toward the global figure when the category has fewer than three peers. The value range reruns the same arithmetic with the category's first and third quartile ratios. Momentum (7-day TVL change, capped), seasoning (age) and breadth (chain count) adjust the headline multiple.
